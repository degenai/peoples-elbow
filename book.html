So What Is The People's Elbow?

The People's Elbow is Alex's own personal project: fundraising and mutual aid, with chair massage at community events to raise money for local causes. It is separate from Elbow Room Massage, his massage practice, and its money is kept apart from Elbow Room's. Paid sessions are booked and paid through Elbow Room.
