How the network works

Alex Adamczyk is a licensed massage therapist who works as a network of residencies across Cherokee County. Each home has its own room, schedule, and character. None of them is a franchise of another, and none of them outranks the rest.

The People's Elbow is the brand behind the work. Hustle House is the standing weekly commitment: the recurring massage day, every Wednesday. Elbow Room Massage Therapy is the clinical business. Cherokee Center for Change is where Alex is privileged to work alongside more than twenty therapists and counselors, the only massage therapist in the building.
