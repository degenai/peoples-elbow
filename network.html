How the network works

Alex Adamczyk is a licensed massage therapist who works as a network of residencies across Cherokee County. Each home has its own room, schedule, and character. None of them is a franchise of another, and none of them outranks the rest.

The People's Elbow is the personal brand that carries the public story: the community appearances, the outreach, and the mutual-aid work. Elbow Room Massage Therapy is the business. Cherokee Center for Change is the practice. Hustle House is the weekly residency.
